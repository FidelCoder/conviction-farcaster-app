# Conviction Markets User Guide

Conviction Markets is a margin and social layer for prediction markets. Users find event markets, review the rules, request leveraged exposure, supply liquidity to vaults, and discuss market calls in Pulse.

## Fast Start

1. Sign in with a supported wallet or smart wallet.
2. Claim a `.viction` profile before using the app fully.
3. Browse markets by topic, region, trend, or search.
4. Open a market to review odds, rules, chart, and resolution details.
5. Request margin, provide vault liquidity, or post a market take on Pulse.
6. Track your trades, signals, vault records, and notifications in Portfolio.

## Key Ideas

### Prediction Markets

A market is a real event question with YES and NO outcomes. The price shows what the market currently implies. For example, YES at 42% means the market is pricing that outcome around 42%.

### Event Rules

Each market has resolution rules. Read them before trading. The rules explain what must happen, the deadline, and what evidence decides the outcome.

### Margin

Margin lets a trader request larger exposure than their own collateral alone. The user chooses a market, YES or NO, collateral amount, leverage, and vault. A request is not the same as a confirmed fill.

### Vault Liquidity

Vaults are the capital layer. Liquidity providers deposit funds that can support margin activity. LPs may earn from that activity, but they also take risk: smart-contract risk, lockup risk, liquidation risk, and market execution risk.

### Pending vs Executed

Pending means the app recorded the intent or transaction flow is not fully executed yet. Executed means the app has a confirmed transaction or execution record. Always check Portfolio for the current state.

## Product Areas

### Home

The landing page explains the product and gives direct entry points into markets, vaults, Pulse, support, and community links.

### Markets

Use Markets to discover event opportunities. You can search, sort, and filter by categories like sports, crypto, politics, finance, tech, geopolitics, culture, weather, regions, and World Cup.

Open a market to see:

- YES and NO prices
- market image and category
- price movement chart
- rules and resolution summary
- related Pulse discussion
- margin request panel
- share options

### Market Details

Market details are where users should slow down. Read the rules, check the deadline, review price movement, then decide whether to request margin or post a signal.

### Margin Request

A margin request lets a trader choose:

- YES or NO
- collateral amount
- leverage level
- vault or chain
- public or private trade visibility where available

After submission, check Portfolio. If the request is pending, it has not fully settled as a market fill yet.

### Vaults

Vaults let liquidity providers deposit capital. Depositors should use vaults as risk capital, not as guaranteed savings.

Vault pages show:

- available vaults
- wallet balance
- deposit flow
- transaction history
- vault collateral records
- supported chains or assets

### Portfolio

Portfolio is the wallet command center. It shows the user profile, wallet balance, active trades, past trades, signals, copy intents, vault records, and transaction hashes when available.

Use Portfolio to confirm what actually happened after posting, depositing, or requesting margin.

### Pulse

Pulse is the social layer for prediction markets. Users can post market takes, attach a market when useful, reply, like, repost, follow traders, and view public profiles.

Pulse is for:

- market calls
- news reactions
- trade ideas
- debate around event outcomes
- public signals
- following strong traders

Users do not always need to attach a market. General posts are allowed when the post is useful to the market community.

### Highlights

Highlights are generated market media and notable market updates. They should be treated as discovery and context, not trading advice.

### Leaderboard

Leaderboard ranks trader activity from recorded platform data. It is useful for finding active users, strong signal posters, and accounts worth following.

### Profiles

Every user should claim a `.viction` identity. The profile is tied to the connected wallet or account session and can include a handle, avatar, bio, and email.

The profile is what other users see in Pulse instead of raw wallet addresses.

### Notifications

Notifications cover position updates, vault activity, social activity, support replies, and product updates. Add an email if you want important updates outside the app.

### Settings

Settings manage profile identity, notification preferences, docs, and account-related options.

### Support

Support has two layers:

- Conviction AI for product questions
- Human support tickets when the issue needs a person

Support tickets can include wallet context and email so the team can follow up. Support mail: convictionsmarket@gmail.com.

### Docs

Docs explain product mechanics, contracts, supported chains, vault behavior, and risk details.

### Telegram Mini App

The Telegram Mini App brings a lighter Conviction experience into Telegram. It is meant for mobile-first access to markets, Pulse, wallet identity, and TON-related liquidity flows.

The Telegram bot can also answer product questions and route support alerts.

## Common Questions

### Is Conviction Markets the same as Polymarket?

No. Conviction uses prediction market data, but adds margin requests, vault liquidity, trader profiles, portfolio tracking, Pulse, support, and community workflows around it.

### Can LP funds lose value?

Yes. Vault liquidity has risk. Funds can be locked while backing active margin, and LPs carry smart-contract, liquidation, market, adapter, and rollout risk.

### Why do I need a `.viction` profile?

It gives the wallet a readable identity across Pulse, portfolio, social actions, support, and public trader profiles.

### Why does a trade show pending?

Pending means the app has not confirmed the final execution or settlement state yet. Check Portfolio for transaction status and history.

### Should I trade only from the market list?

No. Open the full market page first. Review rules, deadline, chart, liquidity, and discussion before requesting margin.

## Best Way To Use The App

1. Start in Markets.
2. Open one event you understand.
3. Read the rules.
4. Check the chart and current YES/NO prices.
5. Post or read Pulse discussion.
6. Request margin only if the setup still makes sense.
7. Track the result in Portfolio.
